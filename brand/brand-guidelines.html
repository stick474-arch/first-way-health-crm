01 · The idea

Named after the medium that lets light travel

Aetherium comes from Aether, the element ancient cosmology said filled the empty space of the universe and let light cross it. That is what we do: we are the invisible channel through which information and work move between clients, the team and the board.

The brand should feel like that medium: calm, clear, quietly precise, with one point of light where attention belongs.

The void

Deep space blues

Abyss and Void: the calm ground every piece starts from.

The aether

The medium

Aether turquoise carries actions, links and the orbit in the logo.

The light

What travels

Amber and aged gold: the A, the photon, the one highlight per view.

How we speak

✓Plain, confident, short. Say what happens: “Log interaction”, “Schedule meeting”, “Quorum met”.
✓Lead with the next step: “Follow up by Friday”.
✓Specific numbers, names and dates.
✕Hype, exclamation marks, emoji.
✕Space clichés: “stellar”, “out of this world”, “to infinity”.
✕Jargon our clients would not use themselves.

02 · Logo system

One mark, three ideas

The official logo fuses three concepts: the amber-gold A in front, the orbit of the Orbital Star around it, and the armillary star behind it in dark blues. The Orbital Star and the armillary stay on as supporting graphics.

The Aetherium mark The official logo: the A in front, the orbit around it, the dark star behind. App icon, avatar, seals, documents.
Source: the orbit A guiding star crossed by one orbit, a photon ahead. Small sizes, social avatars, decorative accents.
Source: the dark star Faceted star, three orbits, a graduated celestial ring. Covers, posters, screens of 160px and up.

Anatomy of the mark

AAmber-gold A. Cast in gold worn by the years: patina, bronze, amber and a glint. It carries the name.
✦Guiding star. Crowns the apex; its long north arm points the way.
◯The orbit. Becomes the crossbar of the A: the aether that carries the work.
•Photon and sparkle. Information in motion, and the people we work with.

03 · Using the logo

Lockups, sizes and space

Preferred · on void or abyss
Aetherium
Amber A, the rest of the name in aged gold.
On light backgrounds
Aetherium
The icon keeps its void tile; the name goes deep blue with the amber A.

Sizes

  • 48px and up: the full mark (A, orbit, dark star, photon).
  • 32–48px: A, crown star and front orbit.
  • Under 32px (favicon): the amber A alone.

Clear space

Keep empty space equal to the star's height on every side of the icon, and half the icon's width between the icon and the name.

Don't

✕Rotate, stretch or outline the logo.
✕Recolor the A or swap the golds for flat yellow.
✕Add glows, drop shadows or busy photo backgrounds.
✕Place it on turquoise or amber fills.

Principles

  • One light per view. If two things glow, neither does.
  • Borders over shadows. Hairlines separate; shadows only for menus, dialogs and toasts.
  • Both themes are first-class. Dark mode lifts the blues and brightens the golds; never just inverts.
  • Motion is slow and linear, like light crossing a distance. No bounces.
